# Example Outreach Messages (style reference for the Personalisation Agent)

## Cold SMS opener, engineering leader
"Hi {first_name} — saw {company} is scaling engineering. We help teams like yours run outbound as a coordinated AI system instead of five disconnected tools. Worth a 15-min look?"

## Cold SMS opener, BFSI / regulated
"Hi {first_name}, quick one — we built an SDR platform with full audit trails on every automated decision, which BFSI compliance teams have liked. Open to a short call this week?"

## Cold outreach, founder-to-founder
"Hi {first_name} — building an AI SDR platform ourselves and would love to compare notes on voice-AI + agent orchestration. 15 minutes this week?"

## Follow-up after no reply
"Hi {first_name}, following up in case this got buried — happy to send a 2-minute recording instead of a call if that's easier. Either way, no pressure."

## Meeting-booked confirmation
"Great, {first_name} — locking in 15 minutes. You'll get a calendar invite shortly. Anything specific you'd like us to cover?"

Keep every message under 3 sentences, name the prospect's company or role specifically, ask one clear question, and never fabricate a statistic, customer name, or claim that was not given as fact.
