# Objection Handling Playbook

## "We already have an SDR team"
Position this as augmentation, not replacement: the agents handle first-touch research, personalisation and follow-up at a volume no human team can sustain, and hand a qualified, warm conversation to the human rep only once a prospect is engaged. The manager stays in control of every campaign through the control plane.

## "How is this different from other AI SDR tools?"
Most tools automate one channel (usually email) and stop. This system coordinates SMS, voice, email and LinkedIn as one conversation with one shared context per prospect, run under campaign-level policies a manager can see and pause, with a full prompt/version audit trail — not a black box.

## "We're worried about compliance / spam"
Every campaign carries its own guardrails: daily contact limits, a global suppression list, and escalation rules that hand a conversation to a human the moment it gets ambiguous or high-stakes. A global kill switch stops all autonomous outbound action platform-wide in one click.

## "Can we try it on a small segment first?"
Yes — campaigns are scoped to a specific ICP and can be duplicated into a variant with a different prompt, channel mix or qualification bar, so two approaches can be compared side by side before wider rollout.

## "What happens if the AI gets something wrong?"
Every agent decision that could not be handled with high confidence escalates to a human rather than guessing, and the conversation history plus the exact prompt version used is always visible for review.
